# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads identify security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements and help the team address them throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical and process risks
- Promote secure-by-default practices
- Coordinate compliance checks and required approvals
- Advise on security controls, exceptions, and mitigation plans
- Confirm security and compliance readiness before release when applicable

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on threat assessment, secure implementation, and remediation
- Work with Product Managers to balance security requirements with product goals and user experience
- Coordinate with Project Managers to track risks, dependencies, approvals, and escalations
- Advise Stakeholders on material security or compliance decisions and residual risk

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA and Test Leads define the quality approach and coordinate validation so that delivered work satisfies acceptance criteria and agreed quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release-readiness criteria
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and end-to-end testing as appropriate
- Validate acceptance criteria and help prioritize defect resolution
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Report test status and unresolved issues clearly

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on testability, test coverage, automation, and defect triage
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and confirm feature behavior
- Coordinate with Project Managers on test milestones, risks, dependencies, and readiness reporting
- Provide Stakeholders with evidence for feature acceptance when manual validation is needed

---

## Release Managers / DevOps Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Leads coordinate the operational path from a completed change to a safe, observable deployment and help maintain reliable delivery systems.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment windows and release readiness checks
- Maintain rollback, recovery, and mitigation plans
- Verify deployment automation, environment readiness, and observability
- Coordinate post-deployment smoke tests and verification
- Monitor release outcomes and capture follow-up actions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to ensure build, deployment, monitoring, and rollback requirements are supported
- Coordinate with QA / Test Leads on release gates and smoke-test results
- Partner with Project Managers on schedules, dependencies, risks, and release communications
- Align with Product Managers and Stakeholders on release scope, timing, customer impact, and announcements

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Partners represent customer and operational feedback during delivery and help the team prepare users and support channels for changes.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer, user, and support-team feedback
- Identify recurring issues, adoption barriers, and customer-impact risks
- Coordinate support readiness, documentation, and enablement needs
- Help assess the impact and urgency of defects or proposed changes
- Share post-release feedback and adoption signals

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Provide Product Managers with customer insight for prioritization, discovery, and success measurement
- Work with Project Managers to raise issues, risks, and dependencies and to coordinate stakeholder updates
- Collaborate with Developers and QA / Test Leads to reproduce and validate customer-reported issues
- Partner with Release Managers / DevOps Leads on customer communications and support readiness

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure that projects can measure outcomes, understand usage, and make evidence-based decisions before and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation requirements, and reporting plans
- Validate data quality and measurement coverage
- Build or coordinate dashboards and outcome reporting
- Analyze adoption, performance, and business impact
- Identify insights that support iteration and continuous improvement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define measurable outcomes and validate business value
- Coordinate with Developers on instrumentation, event definitions, and data quality
- Partner with Project Managers to track progress against milestone and project metrics
- Provide Stakeholders with clear evidence for decisions, status updates, and investment choices

---

## Engineering Managers / Team Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Team Leads support delivery capacity, technical execution health, and team effectiveness while helping translate project commitments into sustainable engineering plans.

### Responsibilities
- Support staffing, capacity planning, and delivery forecasting
- Facilitate technical planning and maintain engineering execution health
- Identify technical constraints, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Coach Developers and support effective collaboration and decision-making
- Help maintain sustainable pace and quality practices

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on capacity, schedules, dependencies, risks, and escalation paths
- Partner with Product Managers on scope, sequencing, trade-offs, and feasibility
- Support Developers with technical direction, reviews, and removal of delivery impediments
- Collaborate with QA / Test Leads and Release Managers / DevOps Leads on quality and operational readiness

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign the relevant persona to a named individual or team for each project so accountability is explicit, even when one person performs multiple roles.
